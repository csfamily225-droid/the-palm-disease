INSTRUCTIONS FOR YOUR TFLITE MODEL:
=====================================
1. Run the Google Colab notebook (colab_training/palm_disease_mobilenetv2_colab.ipynb) on your 5,630 dataset.
2. The notebook will export and automatically download 'palm_model.tflite' and 'labels.txt'.
3. Copy the downloaded 'palm_model.tflite' and 'labels.txt' into this exact folder:
   mobile_app/assets/models/palm_model.tflite
   mobile_app/assets/models/labels.txt
4. Run 'flutter run' on your connected Android or iOS device!
